Chromotherapy

"Colors have frequency. And just like sound, the light of certain colors resonates with our tissues and emotional states. It's a language the body understands without needing words."

What is Chromotherapy (Color Therapy)?

Chromotherapy is a complementary therapy based on the use of specific colors, applied through colored lighting, filters, fabrics or visualizations, to promote energetic, emotional and physical balance. Each color corresponds to a frequency of light that, when absorbed by the body, influences physiological systems, mood and the flow of energy.

Although it is an ancient therapy (found in Ayurvedic, Chinese and Egyptian medicine), science today recognizes that visible light influences hormone production, the circadian rhythm and the autonomic nervous system. Chromotherapy works with these mechanisms intentionally.

Blue and violet are the colors I use most in care and well-being sessions. They act directly on the parasympathetic nervous system, promoting relaxation, reduced inflammation and restorative sleep. It's no coincidence that they run through the entire visual identity of my space.

How I apply chromotherapy

Chromotherapy is always integrated into massage sessions as a complementary resource. I use colored lighting in the room, filters over points on the body and guided visualizations to create an immersive experience aligned with the goal of the session.

The choice of colors is personalized for every session: we talk about how you're feeling emotionally, any physical complaints and what you're looking for, and the color combination is chosen based on that picture.

Benefits of integrated Chromotherapy

  • Enhances the effect of any massage technique
  • Balances the autonomic nervous system
  • Lifts mood and emotional states
  • Support for sleep disturbances
  • Less anxiety and restlessness
  • Encourages the body's self-recovery and immunity
  • A feeling of renewed energy at the end of the session
