The philosophy behind the feet

Foot reflexology is an ancient therapy based on the principle that the feet contain reflex zones corresponding to every organ, gland and system in the body. By working these points with precise pressure, it is possible to stimulate the body's self-regulation and promote balance in areas that are overloaded.

It isn't magic. It's neurology and anatomy. The feet have a high concentration of nerve endings, and pressure on these points sends signals through the nervous system that reach the corresponding organs, promoting relaxation, better function and symptom relief.

Benefits of Foot Reflexology

  • Balance across the body's systems (digestive, hormonal, immune)
  • Less tension and overall stress
  • Better sleep
  • Support for hormonal imbalances
  • Relief from headaches and migraines
  • Better digestion and relief from constipation
  • A feeling of lightness and deep well-being
During the reflexology assessment, the most sensitive points reveal areas of the body that deserve attention. The session also works as an energetic and functional assessment; many clients are surprised by what their feet reveal about the state of their body.

What is the session like?

The session begins with a moment of reconnection: I wash and prepare your feet with natural products. I read the reflex points and then work each zone with targeted pressure, following the complete map of both feet.

The session lasts approximately 50 minutes. Pressure is graduated according to the sensitivity of each point: more sensitive areas indicate a greater need for work, and the pressure is softened as the tension eases.

At the end, there's a feeling of lightness, calm and a different connection with your own body. It's one of the techniques that receives the most positive feedback right from the first session.

When is it especially recommended?

  • The premenstrual phase or hormonal imbalances
  • Intense stress or burnout
  • Functional digestive disorders
  • Insomnia or poor-quality sleep
  • Frequent headaches with no identified organic cause
  • Anyone seeking a holistic, integrative approach
